    Why: The user said not to deploy v2 and to add buying to the live site. The proof section already argues "Say is faster", so the buy panel sits right after the evidence. Instead of: The separate buy page at pay.clible.com/buy, or v2's buy form in the end section. Revisit if: The price or the offer changes ("$5 forever" vs ₹99 / 30 days is still open), or v2/spatial replaces master.

    #2026-10-20 — Keep the device copy of a license, but the worker decides

    Why: The pass shows instantly from localStorage, then /api/verify confirms it. A key the worker never issued is cleared. If the worker is unreachable, the saved copy stays. Instead of: Accounts or email login. Revisit if: Buyers lose keys often. Restoring by payment ID covers that for now.

    #2026-10-17 — Confirm payments by asking Razorpay, not by checking signatures

    Why: The user wanted the simplest possible setup. Fetching the payment with our secret key is just as trustworthy and needs no HMAC code. Instead of: Checking the Checkout signature plus a webhook. Revisit if: Buyers often close the tab before claiming. Then add the payment.captured webhook.
