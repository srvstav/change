    portfolio-site

    My own site should move people. It should be awe-inspiring and quirky, but every word stays plain. The particle intro happens once, at the start. After that the page is about making good things on a small planet.

    #What we built (v2, 2026-10-14)

    • Intro: dots that hesitate and huddle form the James Webb telescope, Voyager 1, a microchip, "all made by people." and then my name. It is slower than clible's intro and less predictable. Returning visitors see only the name.
    • Home: a pull-back from a full Earth to the Pale Blue Dot, one sentence at a time, ending with "It is worth making good things here."
    • Reticle: one targeting reticle. It is the cursor, and the autopilot drives it too.
    • Autopilot: each chapter has its own cue, and it lingers based on how many words are on screen. It waits longer each time you push it away.
    • Work: projects as satellites, plus a horizontal reel. Live Voyager distance and light time, and "you have been here…".
    • Design: deep-space black, bone white, Webb-mirror gold. Bricolage Grotesque, Newsreader italic, Geist Mono.

    #v2.5 (2026-10-14)

    • Speed: a faster autopilot. Long hops rush and then brake; stops are brisk.
    • Prelude is the soundtrack, and the visuals react to it.
    • Fonts: Clash Display for the name and headings, Satoshi for body text. Both are self-hosted.
    • Measured on an M5 Pro: no startup freeze; the tour runs at 60 fps.
    • Tried and removed: a hero reel of eight marvels (V12, Starship, ALMA, Halley VI, Sphere, Opera House, a drone dragon) with drag, click, hold and type play. It is saved in ~/code/name-backup-marvels if we want it back.

    #v3 (2026-10-17, local only, not deployed)

    • Webb and Voyager stay drawn in dots, exactly as live. People love them.
    • Dots become real photos. The new beats are Webb's real mirror, the i9-13900K die, the Starbase catch tower and Artemis I. The photos come from the Tera-Marvel Atlas (leafers.in), copied into public/atlas/. The dots gather into the photo's shape and colours, then the sharp 2560 px photo resolves in and breaks back into dots.
    • Tried and dropped (2026-10-16): turning photos into dots only. 72k dots can't carry a photo's detail, so it looked like a toy. The real photo has to appear.
    • Smooth transitions. Each dot goes to the matching place in the next shape. Every shape is built in a Web Worker.
    • Earth is real (Apollo 17 → Voyager's actual Pale Blue Dot frame). Type: Zodiak, Mona Sans, Martian Mono.
    • Tests: test/stress.mjs, test/intro-perf.mjs.

    #Next

    1. v3 saved on branch v3-real-photos (commit eea0b32, not pushed). Start next session from ~/code/name/HANDOFF.md.
    2. Put in my real email (src/content.ts).
    3. Deploy, then check FPS on a real phone.
    4. Check the Voyager distance against NASA's live page.
