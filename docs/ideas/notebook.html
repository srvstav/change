    notebook

    #The announcement

    "My ideas live in Apple Notes, Samsung Notes, Obsidian and Google Keep. notebook pulls them all into one place, merges the same idea written in different apps, and every morning tells me the 3 ideas worth my time today, why, and the first step. When I open an idea, I see every scrap I've ever written about it and keep writing right there."

    #Problem

    Ideas are scattered across four notes apps and several Obsidian vaults. Finding anything means opening each one. Ideas get lost or rewritten from scratch.

    #Insight

    The apps don't need to sync. One read-only index over all of them is enough to find, merge and rank ideas. The value is in the merge and the ranking, not the search.

    #Done when

    I open notebook instead of the notes apps when I sit down to work on an idea, for a whole week.

    #Time box

    One week to daily use.

    #Riskiest assumption

    That an AI model can be called cheaply and reliably enough. The free Gemini tier ran out partway through reading 263 notes. Test first: a paid key, or a small local model.

    #Not doing

    • Writing back into any notes app.
    • Chat, email, shopping, WhatsApp sources (parsers exist on branch more-sources).

    #What we learned (2026-10-20)

    • Search and lists alone felt useless. The useful parts are: merged ideas, a daily pick of 3 with a first step, and one page per idea to write on.
    • Keep and Samsung Notes have no API for personal notes. Exports only.
    • My notes are split across three overlapping systems (change, ΩNOMNIA, Frontier-Builder-Vault). Merging them may help more than any tool.
