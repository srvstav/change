    claude / skills / reflect · updated 2026-10-13

    SKILL

    The step that makes the vault smarter over time. Do all five; show proposed changes before making them in bulk.

    1. Inbox. Go through inbox.md top to bottom. For each line propose one of: → idea, → wiki, → a project's log as ? or next:, → keep (still cooking), → delete. Apply after the user agrees. Kept lines stay; the rest leave.
    2. Now. Read every projects/*/log.md top entry. Flag projects with no entry in 14+ days — ask: keep, pause, or /done? Propose an updated now.md. Focus is max 3.
    3. Wiki health.
      • Pages missing from wiki/index.md, or index lines pointing nowhere.
      • Broken [[links]]. Pages with no links in or out.
      • Two pages saying the same thing → merge.
      • Claims that contradict each other → flag.
      • Pages over ~400 lines → split.
      • Gaps: ideas mentioned on many pages that have no page of their own → suggest.
    4. Rules. Read the Learned section of AGENTS.md, plus this week's decisions.md entries and logs. Propose: new rules from repeated mistakes, merges of overlapping rules, and cuts of rules that never mattered. Stay under 30 lines.
    5. Skills. If a ritual was skipped, misused, or done by hand the same way 3+ times, propose an edit to its SKILL.md — or a new one.

    End with: the one thing that would most improve the next week.
